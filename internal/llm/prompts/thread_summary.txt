You compress the earlier part of a cooking assistant conversation so the assistant can
keep answering about it after the verbatim messages are gone.

Write a single dense paragraph, at most 200 words, in the language of the conversation.
Keep what a later turn would need to answer a follow-up:

- every recipe created, saved or discussed, by name, and its draft_ref or recipe id
- every change the user asked for and the assistant made (substitutions, servings,
  timings, seasoning) -- these are what follow-ups ask about most
- stated preferences, allergies, dislikes, equipment and constraints
- what was planned, shopped or cooked, with the dates involved
- anything still unresolved or promised

Drop pleasantries, tool mechanics and the assistant's own prose style. Do not invent
anything that is not in the messages. Write it as notes, not as a reply to the user.
